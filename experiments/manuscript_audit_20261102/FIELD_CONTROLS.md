# HouseZero photovoltaic control windows

Before counting any equality runs in the downloaded photovoltaic readings, define a separate physical control for legitimate zero or stable power. Use one target, PV meter 1. Take every local midnight to 02:59 in the published year. Require 180 consecutive minutes, complete timestamps and finite values on the target, PV meter 2 and site solar radiation. Exclude daylight-saving clock changes. Require radiation from 0 to 1 W/m² throughout and absolute PV meter 2 readings at most 0.1 kW throughout. Require at least 30 daytime minutes (09:00–15:59 local) on both the preceding and following local dates with radiation at least 150 W/m² and absolute power at least 0.5 kW on both meters. Do not fill gaps. The daylight requirements verify response around the window, without conditioning on the target's nighttime constancy. Include all passing windows. Report the target's longest exact-equality run and count windows with runs of at least 2, 10, 30, 60 and 180 minutes. This is a control for a stationary physical regime on the released minute series. It cannot independently certify the target meter's internal state minute by minute, supply a fault-positive example or estimate workload on BDG2 electricity consumption.
