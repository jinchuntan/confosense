# Derived support and schedule evidence

This authorized review publication adds derived row identities, role memberships, eligible observed target values, fixed hypothetical fault schedules and support/resource-planning tables. It performs no model fitting and does not publish the local raw archives, prepared pickle caches or environment. The observation columns permit independent verification of changed/null realizations; the source archives and feature implementation are still required for future fitting.

PLEIA source: Antonio Martínez Ibarra, Aurora González-Vidal and Antonio Skarmeta Gómez, *PLEIAData: consumption, HVAC (Heating, Ventilation & Air Conditioning), temperature, weather and motion sensor data for smart buildings applications*, [Zenodo7620136](https://zenodo.org/records/7620136), DOI10.5281/zenodo.7620136, [CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the [verified rights metadata](../pleia_rights_metadata.json). Modified derivatives retain the existing room/energy-meter choice and causal preprocessing; they are not the unmodified source archive.

RICO source: Z. Thiry, M. Ruocco, A. Nocente and O.A. Oksavik (2025), *The RICO dataset: A multivariate HVAC indoors and outdoors time-series dataset*, Data in Brief61:111678, DOI10.1016/j.dib.2025.111678; [official Zenodo14871584](https://zenodo.org/records/14871584), DOI10.60609/tw79-4k72, [CC BY4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Derivatives apply the existing quality-flag/run segmentation, target selection, causal preprocessing, feature eligibility, versioned chronological memberships and synthetic sensor transformations. No source-author endorsement is implied.

The BDG2 additions here are no-fit forecasting membership/identity tables and planning metadata for the unchanged ten-building cohort.
