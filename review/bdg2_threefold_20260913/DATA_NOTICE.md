# BDG2 three-fold derived evidence

The newly published fold-1 and fold-0 observations, memberships, synthetic events, forecasts, intervals, alert streams and derived results use the same ten-building BDG2 source and transformations described in the [BDG2 source attribution and license notice](../bdg2_operational_pilot_20260913/DATA_NOTICE.md). These derived data are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 with that attribution. This does not replace the repository software license.

The source is Miller et al. (2020), *The Building Data Genome Project 2, energy meter data from the ASHRAE Great Energy Predictor III competition*, Scientific Data 7, 368, DOI 10.1038/s41597-020-00712-x. Additional changes here are fitted quantile models and operational evaluation on the two frozen later historical periods, plus count pooling and exploratory paired analysis. The faults are simulated; the original authors do not endorse the results. Original fold-2 evidence and notices remain preserved.

Raw source archives, other buildings, local prepared caches, environments and external backups are outside this publication. Oversized CSVs are losslessly split for publication and can be restored to their exact original filenames and SHA-256 values using the indexed manifest and reconstruction command.
