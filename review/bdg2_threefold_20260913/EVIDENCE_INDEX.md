## Recompute without fitting

From `smart_building_conformal`, using the existing compatible NumPy/pandas environment and fresh audit output directories:

```powershell
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/verify_bdg2_materialize.py --manifest outputs/amendment004/bdg2_pilot_publication_v1/large_files.json
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/verify_bdg2_materialize.py --manifest outputs/amendment004/bdg2_threefold_f1_publication_v1/large_files.json
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/verify_bdg2_materialize.py --manifest outputs/amendment004/bdg2_threefold_f0_publication_v1/large_files.json
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/independent_bdg2_operational_audit.py --run outputs/amendment004/bdg2_threefold_f1_s42_v1 --out outputs/amendment004/reviewer_fold1_audit
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/independent_bdg2_operational_audit.py --run outputs/amendment004/bdg2_threefold_f0_s42_v1 --out outputs/amendment004/reviewer_fold0_audit
& C:/cfs_venv/Scripts/python.exe -B scripts/combine_bdg2_threefold.py --manifest outputs/amendment004/bdg2_threefold_frozen_v2/execution_analysis_manifest.json --out outputs/amendment004/reviewer_threefold_combination
```

Reconstruction verifies every part and original SHA-256, refuses mismatched existing files and never overwrites a canonical result. The combined helper uses the published independent-audit tables; separate reviewer audit outputs let those tables be checked again. The generalized [independent audit](../../smart_building_conformal/scripts/independent_bdg2_operational_audit.py) does not import production metric or selection code. The [combined helper](../../smart_building_conformal/scripts/combine_bdg2_threefold.py) retains the explicitly frozen model-seed scope `[42]` and original-building pairing. The [normal completed-resume verifier](../../smart_building_conformal/scripts/verify_bdg2_completed_resume.py) additionally requires the exact evaluated source/configuration and local raw data; partial-stage recovery is not claimed.

The [current evidence index](../CURRENT_EVIDENCE.md) and [panel response status](../../PANEL_RESPONSE_MATRIX.md) distinguish this bounded operational benchmark from the preserved forecasting pilot and the still-incomplete broader dissertation study.
