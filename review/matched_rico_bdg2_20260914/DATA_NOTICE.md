# Derived RICO and BDG2 matched-forecast evidence

This authorized review publication contains derived observations, calibration/test predictions, learned model artifacts, chronological membership and computational measurements. These are modified derivatives of the existing public datasets. No raw HDF archives, prepared pickle caches, credentials or local environment are published. No source-author endorsement is implied.

RICO: Z. Thiry, M. Ruocco, A. Nocente and O. A. Oksavik (2025), *The RICO dataset: A multivariate HVAC indoors and outdoors time-series dataset*, Data in Brief 61:111678, DOI 10.1016/j.dib.2025.111678; [Zenodo 14871584](https://zenodo.org/records/14871584), DOI 10.60609/tw79-4k72, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This version retains B.RTD3, original quality/run selection, causal preprocessing and the published amendment-005 whole-run memberships.

BDG2: source attribution and [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) are retained in the [existing BDG2 notice](../bdg2_threefold_20260913/DATA_NOTICE.md). The new BDG2 source-derived observations, predictions, membership and statistical result tables are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0. They retain the ten-building cohort and original hourly electricity target, with causal preprocessing and matched flat/sequence eligibility. This publication adds persistence/XGBoost/Attention-LSTM forecasts and their own-model intervals; it does not replace the historical operational benchmark.

The reused PLEIA evidence retains its [existing attribution and license notice](../support_design_20260913/DATA_NOTICE.md). Repository software terms remain distinct from dataset-derived material.
