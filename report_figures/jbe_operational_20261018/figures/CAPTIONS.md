# Figure captions

## Section 4.2: alert tradeoffs

Operational tradeoffs across interval and alert configurations. (a,b) PLEIAData temperature and energy at a 10-minute forecast horizon and nominal 95% coverage, using the combined numerical and availability stream. Each panel contains four interval controls evaluated under five aggregation rules. Detection is conditional on the 68 recorded historical contexts per target and uses the seed, context and disturbance-stratum weighting in Section 3.5. (c) BDG2 at a one-hour horizon and nominal 95% coverage: uncalibrated quantiles and static, periodic and rolling CQR under the three evaluated aggregation rules (33 configurations). (d) BDG2 periodic CQR updated every 12 hourly observations, with 1-of-1 aggregation and nominal coverage targets of 95%, 97.5%, 99% and 99.5%. BDG2 values are means of the 15 chronological-evaluation and seed results, evaluated using the same eligible observations across configurations. Horizontal axes use logarithmic scales. Marker shapes identify the interval control; selected annotations identify aggregation rules or coverage targets. Marker colour indicates restricted mean detection time in minutes, with separate scales for PLEIAData (limit 110 minutes) and BDG2 (limit 360 minutes). Only the line in (d) connects results, to show the sequence of nominal coverage targets. Panels retain their distinct detection definitions and should be interpreted within each monitoring setting. All results are descriptive.

## Section 4.3: coverage recovery

Observed coverage recovery after the severity-two level shift under static, periodic and rolling calibration. Blue segments show the proportion of cases meeting the recovery criterion; hatched segments show cases without observed recovery before follow-up ended. Labels give observed recoveries divided by assessed cases. Recovery is the first complete post-disturbance rolling window with coverage against the retained clean reference within five percentage points of its pre-disturbance value. Each case is one building or experimental run within a chronological evaluation and model seed. Cases without observed recovery remain in the denominator and are right-censored. Follow-up duration and group structure differ between settings; the figure is a descriptive comparison within each setting.
